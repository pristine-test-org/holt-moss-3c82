How we keep one small list tidy: three habits that came out of a season of using Holt Moss every day.

Every item gets a single owner, a clear title and a finish line, and anything that sits untouched for two weeks is either done or deleted.

5items shown at a time
14days before a review
1owner per item

Three habits

  • Write the title as the result you want, not the task.
  • Check items off the moment they are done.
  • Clear the list on Friday so Monday starts fresh.
